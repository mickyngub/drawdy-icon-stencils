# Icon Stencils

Every [Lucide](https://lucide.dev) line icon, one drag away from your Drawdy board. Use them to wireframe screens, sketch interfaces and make diagrams readable at a glance.

![Searching Icon Stencils for menu, search and user icons and dragging them into a phone inbox wireframe on a Drawdy board](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mickyngub/drawdy-icon-stencils/main/docs/media/icon-stencils.gif)

## What it's for

- **Wireframes and UI sketches**: menus, arrows, text formatting, devices, file types, accounts and more, all in one consistent line style.
- **Flows and diagrams** that need clear, simple symbols.
- **Easier-to-read boards**: an icon next to a note or a heading helps people find things fast.

## How to use

1. Click the **Icon Stencils** button on the right of the board.
2. Pick a category: the ones most used for UI work come first (Layout, Arrows, Text formatting, Shapes, Accounts & access, Devices, File icons), then every other Lucide category. Or type in the search box; press `/` to jump to it.
3. Drag an icon onto the board and let go where you want it. Or click it to add it in the middle of your view; more clicks line icons up in rows instead of stacking them.
4. In the search box, press Enter to add the top result.

## Options

- **Size**: S, M, L or XL, from 32 to 256 board units.
- **Line weight**: Thin, Regular or Bold.
- **Label**: also add the icon's name, centred under it and grouped with the icon so they move together.
- **Colour**: Ink (the board's foreground colour), four Drawdy colours, or any colour you pick.

## Tips

- Your last 12 icons stay in a **Recent** row, and the palette remembers your options.
- Icons are vector images, so they stay sharp however far you zoom in.

## Privacy

- The icons download from jsDelivr as you scroll. Nothing you draw or type is sent anywhere.
- Your recent icons and options are saved in this browser, so they are the same on every board you open here.
