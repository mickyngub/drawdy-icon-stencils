# Changelog

## 1.0.5

- a crisper version of the icon, shown on the marketplace card and on the extension's button

## 1.0.4

- the panel now uses Drawdy's own colours and font and matches the other icon pickers
- category chips scroll with a fade instead of being cut off, and selected options are grey instead of lime

## 1.0.3

- a short GIF on the listing page that shows searching for icons and dragging them into a wireframe in action

## 1.0.2

- a label sits centred under its icon, and an icon with a label is placed as one group, so the two move together

## 1.0.1

- a clearer listing page: what the extension is for, how to use it, tips and privacy, in plain words

## 1.0.0

First public release

- UI and wireframe line icons from Lucide, grouped by Lucide's categories in one scrollable chip row, with All for every icon
- Ranked search inside the selected category or across every icon, where every word must match, 120 results at a time with Show more, `/` to search and Enter to add the first result
- Drag an icon to where you let go, or click to add it in view; repeated clicks line up in rows instead of stacking
- Size S to XL, a colour, a stroke width and an optional name label
- Icons keep their proportions and stay sharp when you zoom in
- A Recent row with the last 12 icons you placed; the options are remembered in this browser
